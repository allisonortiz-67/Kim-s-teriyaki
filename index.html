心 • Our Story

Bringing Heartfelt Teriyaki to the Kent Community

Located on Central Avenue North in the heart of Kent, Washington, Kim’s Teriyaki is dedicated to the authentic Pacific Northwest teriyaki tradition. We believe comfort food should never cut corners.

Each morning, we prepare our signature teriyaki sauce from scratch—balancing aged soy, ginger, garlic, and natural sweetness—and marinate premium cuts of chicken and beef so every bite caramelizes beautifully over our open grill.
