心 • Welcome to Kim’s Teriyaki

A Kent Tradition of Flame-Grilled Comfort

Welcome to Kim’s Teriyaki, proudly serving the Kent community from our kitchen on Central Avenue North. Rooted in the beloved Pacific Northwest teriyaki craft, we focus on timeless recipes prepared with care every single day.

From our overnight-marinated chicken and prime beef to our slow-simmered house teriyaki glaze and crisp panko katsu, every dish is cooked fresh when you walk through our doors or call ahead. Whether you are stopping in for a quick weekday lunch or bringing dinner home to the family, you can always count on generous portions and genuine hospitality.
